What to Expect

How Tryouts Work

Tryouts are held every fall and span multiple days. Both competitive squad and practice squad spots are available.

What We Look For
  • Technical ability — first touch, range, and composure on the ball
  • Tactical awareness — shape, pressing, and decision-making
  • Athletic edge — pace, stamina, and physical presence
  • Coachability — listens, adapts, and puts the team first
Missed Tryouts?

If you missed fall tryouts, contact us to organize a private tryout. We only conduct these during the off-season (winter, spring). Practice squad spots are always available with no cuts.
