What to Expect

How Tryouts Work

Tryouts are held each fall across multiple days, with both competitive-squad and practice-squad opportunities available.

What We Look For
  • Technical ability — first touch, range, and composure on the ball
  • Tactical awareness — shape, pressing, and decision-making
  • Athletic edge — pace, stamina, and physical presence
  • Coachability — listens, adapts, and puts the team first
Missed Tryouts?

If you missed fall tryouts, reach out and we can arrange a private evaluation during the off-season. Practice squad spots remain available throughout the year, and there are no cuts.
